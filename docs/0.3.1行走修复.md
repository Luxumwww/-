# 0.3.1 行走修复

## 问题与处理

0.3 的运行时腿部裁切和骨骼变形会在腿根产生切口、阶梯边缘和贴片，部分帧脚掌也会变形。之前对落脚顺序与边界的测试没有覆盖这些视觉缺陷，用户反馈后逐帧放大确认了问题。

这次删除 `CatWalkRig` 和 `CatGait`，改为八张完整猫图的行走循环。运行时只绘制整个 PNG，四肢不再单独裁切、弯折或拉伸。漫步、追鼠标、找饭和叼鼠标时共用这一组完整行走帧。

- 每帧使用 512×308 固定画布，关闭自动裁透明边，避免各帧被缩放成不同大小。
- 只整体移动取图区域，对齐脚底；不编辑身体部位。
- 一个循环对应 64 个未缩放移动像素。实际移动才推进帧，停止后保持当前帧。
- 左转通过整张图片镜像完成。
- 行走时嘴的位置按新图重新标定，叼鼠标仍跟随嘴部。

这是一组稍僵硬的简化行走帧。各帧之间仍有轻微姿态与花纹差异，不能视为精确的真实猫步态模拟。

## 图像生成提示词

以下为实际提交给内置图像生成工具的完整提示词。第三次的输入是第二次图集和原始站立参考图。

### 第一次：四列两行（未采用）

```text
Generate a production-ready transparent PNG sprite sheet containing exactly EIGHT COMPLETE FULL-BODY photographic walking poses of the SAME strange black-and-white cat, 4 columns by 2 rows. Read row-major from upper left through upper right, then lower left through lower right. Image 1 is the authoritative original cat character: black ear cap, black saddle markings, white narrow face, contemptuous narrow eyes, tiny wedge head, exceptionally lanky long thin body and thin long legs, long black tail. Image 2 is only the side-on proportion and markings reference. Preserve the SAME cat proportions and markings across all eight poses. No ordinary cute round cat, no short thick legs.
All 8 are complete coherent photographic cutouts, each with four naturally attached legs, knees/elbows/hocks and whole paws. Every leg root flows into the body with continuous fur, NO torn edges or gaps, no sliced pieces, no detached legs, no exposed cut lines, no duplicated limbs. Anatomically feline gait, not four rubber poles.
LAYOUT: exactly 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows with generous transparent gaps, no text, no borders. Within EVERY cell cat faces RIGHT in the exact same strict side view, fixed body size, fixed shoulder/hip/head coordinates, fixed baseline. Same lighting and fur texture. Every cat including tail and whiskers comfortably inside its own cell. The cat takes about 86% of cell width, with equal empty margin left/right. Keep neck and face almost stationary, torso horizontal and thin. Head relatively small and low, detached-looking forward neck attitude but neck physically connected. Four separately visible legs with natural overlap and depth. Near fore lower leg has the dark marking, far hind lower leg also dark as in the reference.
ANIMATION: one measured walking cycle with staggered four-foot steps, hind foot followed by fore foot on the same side, then other side. NO same-side fore and hind paws swinging forward simultaneously. Most frames have three feet supporting weight.
Pose 1 (top left): near hind foot just landed forward under hip, near fore is planted farther back, far hind planted behind, far fore finishing its step.
Pose 2: near hind supports, near fore lifts and swings forward with a gently bent elbow; other legs support.
Pose 3: near fore lands forward under shoulder, near hind supports slightly behind; far hind begins lifting.
Pose 4: far hind swings forward bent at knee and hock; near fore and the other two paws support.
Pose 5: far hind lands forward under hip; near fore support now slightly behind; far fore begins lifting.
Pose 6: far fore swings forward bent at elbow; other three paws support, near hind is planted behind.
Pose 7: far fore lands forward under shoulder; near hind begins lifting behind.
Pose 8: near hind swings forward with natural knee/hock bend, preparing to return to Pose 1, other paws support.
Use modest step amplitude and modest paw lift. Long legs keep constant anatomical length; flex at joints during swing, not shorten or bend like noodles. Bodies, back patches, tails, faces should remain almost identical between poses; only leg positions change. Keep all foot soles and fur complete, avoid foot cropping. All poses strictly SIDE VIEW; never turn toward camera. Photorealistic fur cutout matching original reference, not cartoon. Actually TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND; alpha zero outside cat, NO glow, NO halo, NO drop shadow, NO ground plane, NO labels or annotations. Clean continuous silhouettes. This will be displayed unmodified as whole animation frames; the limbs will NOT be warped by code.
```

### 第二次：清理边缘与留白（未采用）

```text
Edit the supplied EIGHT-FRAME walking sprite sheet. Preserve exactly the same cat, all eight complete feline poses, fur markings, long slender body and legs, small contemptuous head, photographic style, direction and row-major sequence. DO NOT redesign anatomy or redraw the cat into a cute pet.
Fix ONLY the sprite-sheet production defects:
1. Put each COMPLETE cat comfortably inside its own equal cell with at least 8 percent genuinely empty transparent margin on left and right and 10 percent above and below. There are exactly 4 columns and 2 rows. Equal cell dimensions. Same cat size and same body/head/hip anchors in each cell. The tail of the first column and whiskers of the last column must never touch outer sheet edges or the neighboring cell.
2. Remove EVERY disconnected white/gray/color speck, dust, haze, glow, shadow, noise, or alpha island in the transparent areas, particularly under the belly and between the legs. Alpha ZERO everywhere outside the actual cat fur, whiskers and silhouette. Not a black background. No glow or halo. The apparent background must be actual clean transparency.
3. Keep all FOUR legs and their whole paws in each pose; keep shoulders and hips naturally attached with continuous fur. Never cut off legs or alter the pose sequence. Keep the cat body as one coherent photographic object; no slices or transparent tears inside a limb.
Output a clean transparent PNG, 4-column 2-row evenly aligned sprite sheet, no labels, no guide lines. Favor faithful identical cat bodies and accurate attached limbs over decorative polish. Do not add any other content.
```

### 第三次：两列四行完整帧（采用）

```text

Keep the body, head, hip anchors and common foot baseline at the same relative position in all cells. All cats whole and entirely contained, including every paw, tail tip and whisker. No touching cell edges, no crossing between cells, no neighboring tail fragments. Big visibly empty full-height transparent gutter between the two columns; visibly empty horizontal transparent gutters between all four rows. No visible grid lines, no labels or text.
Pose order: row1 gets old poses1/2, row2 gets old poses3/4, row3 gets old poses5/6, row4 gets old poses7/8. Preserve their complete, naturally attached legs and photographic fur. Whole legs and shoulders/hip fur connected with no cracks, no cut lines, no stretched noodles. Cats are complete unmodified objects, NEVER body part collages.

```
