# 素材生成记录

## 保留素材

- 工具：内置 imagegen，透明背景开启；没有使用 API / CLI 后备路径。
- 输入：五张原图作为同一只猫的角色参考。站姿作为脸部与花纹基准，chew 作为长躯干、伸颈姿态基准。
- 最终素材：`assets/generated/transitions-atlas.png`，2172 × 724，RGBA。
- 六个姿态：醒来低伏、半起身、步态 A、步态 B、趴下、接近 rag。
- 素材清单：`assets/character.json`。生成图未严格贴合等分网格，程序按实测 `source` 矩形取帧，保留脚尖和尾巴。
- 五张核心状态使用 `assets/reference` 原图；补帧不替代角色关键状态。
- 程序在渲染时对原图做连续采样以生成头部跟踪、伸颈与下颌微动，不修改原始 PNG。

## 最终采用的生成提示词

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent desktop-pet animation atlas for Windows, photographic cutout texture. All FIVE inputs are reference images of ONE SAME CAT, not separate cats. Input 1 standing contempt is main identity, input 2 stretched-neck confrontation is body-proportion reference, input 3 curled sleep, input 4 rag, input 5 hiss. Produce ONE transparent PNG atlas, exactly 3 columns and 2 rows with SIX evenly spaced complete full-body cat poses, every cat facing screen-right, centered inside its own equal rectangular cell with at least 8% transparent margin. NO text, NO labels, NO lines, NO ground shadows or props. Every pose same camera as standing and chew reference, same coat pattern, same physical scale measured by torso length. Row1 col1: waking up from curled sleep, head lifted but torso crouched low, ears flat and irritated. Row1 col2: awkward halfway rising on very long thin legs, small narrow head, contemptuous half-closed eyes. Row1 col3: first walking keyframe, long thin near-side front leg extended forward, hind leg behind, long stretched horizontal torso, head small, flat back. Row2 col1: opposing walking keyframe, near-side front leg behind, opposite leg forward, SAME body height and camera as preceding walk. Row2 col2: lowering from standing, skinny front legs folding and very long torso descending toward floor, head pointing forward low. Row2 col3: lying almost flat with head turned away, before the final rag reference. Mandatory identity: photographic black and white cat with very thin elongated body, four long skinny legs, small narrow wedge head, black ear caps separated by white forehead stripe, pale gray-green half-closed contemptuous eyes, pink nose, white muzzle, black tail, dorsal black patches aligned to standing reference and small black patch at bottom of near foreleg. Preserve weird creepy neurotic awkward unappealing proportions. No cute gestures, no round face, no shortened legs, no large head, no cartoon makeover, no gum or food, no extra limbs. These are intermediate animation frames of exactly the supplied animal. Genuine alpha-transparent background.
```

## 音效

`assets/audio/growl.wav` 与 `hiss.wav` 为项目内 `WaveClip.CreateDemoSounds` 产生的合成占位音效，非真实猫叫录音。低吼以低频基音、谐波、呼吸起伏和滤波噪声构成，哈气以短促噪声包络构成。没有外部音频版权来源。
